AREAL PROJECTION SENSITIVITY: SAVED REPLICATE 1

The equal-vertex projector has measurable mesh sensitivity in this realization. Exact integration of the same piecewise-linear finite-element (FE) surface is closer to the stored areal truth under both diagnostic nodal reconstructions. This is evidence of a quadrature approximation effect, not proof that projection caused the fitted areal variance change.

RMSE versus stored true areal averages (25 areas)
                                  Production mesh    Finer mesh
Bilinear nodal values, equal A_B       0.041597          0.032468
Bilinear nodal values, exact FE        0.016744          0.011648
Nearest-cell nodal values, equal A_B   0.043780          0.038946
Nearest-cell nodal values, exact FE    0.029919          0.022188

The production mesh has 1676 vertices and 36-44 equal-weight vertices per area. The finer mesh has 2832 vertices and 65-76 vertices per area.

With bilinear nodal values, the RMS change in areal averages between meshes is 0.047745 for equal-vertex A_B and 0.010120 for exact FE integration. The equal-minus-exact FE discrepancy has RMS 0.041405 on the production mesh and 0.031827 on the finer mesh. The largest production equal-minus-truth error is 0.124188. This falls to a maximum 0.072793 on the finer mesh, although individual blocks do not all improve.

For context, the generating areal-error SD is sqrt(0.1)=0.316228 and this replicate's realized areal-error RMS is 0.307628. Equal-A_B discrepancy MSE is approximately 0.0011-0.0019 across these reconstructions and meshes, much smaller than 0.1. Its size cannot be directly translated into a change in the fitted observation variance because the model jointly estimates a spatial field, covariance, and observation variances. A much larger fitted-variance shift therefore remains an optimization/identifiability question as well as a discretization question.

What was verified
- All 25 saved true areal means equal the corresponding 100-cell raster means to 6.7e-16
- Both equal-vertex A_B matrices have unit row sums to floating-point tolerance
- No mesh vertex belongs to more than one areal polygon in these particular saved meshes
- The exact FE projector covers every polygon completely and integrates constant, x and y functions exactly to numerical tolerance
- The response truth, true/observed areal values, mesh coordinates, and saved A_B are identical across the corresponding baseline Melding, baseline PSmelding, delta=2 Melding, and delta=2 PSmelding fits for each mesh

Interpretation limits
The continuous Gaussian field was simulated only at 2500 cell centres. Nearest-cell and bilinear interpolation reconstruct nodal values for this diagnostic; neither provides unobserved true continuous-field values at mesh vertices. Coordinates beyond the outer cell centres are clamped to the nearest centre. Some triangle vertices outside the unit domain contribute to FE integration of boundary-crossing triangles, so this clamping is an explicit boundary extension assumption.

Equal A_B minus exact FE averages isolates quadrature weighting conditional on the chosen nodal values. Exact FE minus stored truth combines interpolation, boundary extension and finite-element representation error. These components can partly cancel; their squared errors must not simply be added. One field and two mesh resolutions do not establish convergence or simulation-wide accuracy.

No model refits were performed and no original files were changed. The exact FE projector is retained only as a diagnostic alternative. Adopting it in production would be a deliberate change from the current equal-vertex implementation and should first be checked with controlled refits.
