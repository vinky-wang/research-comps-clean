Driver review
- investigation/run_optimizer.R correctly reuses each saved formula environment, SPDE, projector, data, likelihood family, priors, quadrature and variance adjustment
- Starts use the named internal Gaussian areal log precision, setting eta=-log(tau2_B)
- control.mode restart=TRUE and fixed=FALSE restart optimization rather than treating hyperparameters as known
- Latent initial x is held at the original saved conditional mode, consistently within each source fit
- The fitted-value prediction indices match the original engine extraction
- Full new objects are retained separately and original bundles are not replaced
- Local documentation confirms tolerance controls hyperparameter optimization f/g/x; tolerance.step still defaults to .001
- Gradient step h=.005, inner latent tolerance and posterior integration strategy stay unchanged
- Thus these are controlled hyperparameter-optimization checks, not a claim that all numerical approximations have been tightened
- Compare log posterior mode only within identical data, mesh, model and parameterization
- A positive saved cov.intern is not proof that the numerical Hessian was originally positive definite: INLA repairs the Hessian
- mode_status=0 does not outweigh a logged negative eigenvalue or an integration point with higher density than the saved mode

These differences are not post-processing errors, reciprocal-of-mean mistakes or stale-summary artifacts

- nevertheless areal variance mean .039699745 versus .017304971 and interval widths differ
- repaired Hessian has tied eigenvalues approximately 8.861 and 8.861

- asymmetric stdev corrections differ greatly across those directions
- timing-based predictor strategy differs: strength baseline chooses plain arithmetic, areal-noise baseline chooses data-rich arithmetic; both choose serial Qx arithmetic
- identical R seeds and thread counts do not freeze this timing-selected arithmetic path

- evidence is consistent with unstable direction-dependent integration after Hessian repair, with an insufficiently resolved optimum; not simply two different well-separated reported optima
- use controlled runs to test that explanation rather than claiming it is proven solely by the saved outputs
