Limits that keep numerical acceptance pending
- It covers only gamma=1, global replicate 1, Melding and PSmelding. It does
  not assess the problematic clustering dataset or multiple initializations.
- Matching inputs are not a numerical-result pass: no tolerance is enforced
  for parameter or score differences. The script calculates mean shifts but
  does not gate medians, interval endpoints, spatial prediction differences,
  covariance changes or needs_review diagnostics. Both retained laptop-1
  baseline fits have needs_review=TRUE and a Hessian-related flag.
- Source identity omits the checker itself; the explicit checksum closes
  that transfer gap. Runtime identity omits native-library/binary checksums,
  global INLA options and some loaded suggested packages. Review session
  details, including sn, numDeriv, MatrixModels and mnormt, plus BLAS/LAPACK
  and sf's external-library versions.
- Successful startup, matching architecture, matching hashes, and even close
  results on this baseline do not prove posterior accuracy or full-study
  readiness. Review numerical differences against the documented acceptance
  criteria and the separate initialization-sensitivity evidence.
