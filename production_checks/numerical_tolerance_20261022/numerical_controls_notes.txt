Verified effective defaults and behavior
- control.inla$tolerance=.005. Unless separately supplied, tolerance.f is
  2*tolerance and tolerance.g/tolerance.x equal tolerance. Therefore setting
  only tolerance=1e-4 gives f=2e-4 and g=x=1e-4.
- tolerance.step=.001 controls the inner latent-field optimization. Changing
  outer tolerance does not change it. Logs also report a separate inner
  tolerance_func=.0005; it is not a documented control.inla argument here.
- h=.005; num.gradient="central"; num.hessian="central". The backend maps h
  to gradient step h and Hessian step sqrt(abs(h)); original logs therefore
  report Hessian step .0707107. Changing h is not a Hessian-only intervention.
- optimise.strategy="smart"; use.directions=TRUE; restart=0. The help labels
  direction use and optimizer strategy experimental. restart counts further
  optimizer restarts at a found optimum; it is distinct from control.mode.
- control.compute$internal.opt=TRUE enables internal online optimizations.
  The backend times computational implementations and chooses between plain
  and data-rich predictor evaluation. FALSE bypasses these comparisons,
  fixes predictor=plain and Qx=serial, and fixes the sort cutoff to 128.
- int.strategy="auto"; dz=.75; diff.logdens=6; force.diagonal=FALSE;
  diagonal=0; cmin=-Inf. These original fits use CCD (4 or 5 hyperparameters).
  Their logs label dz/diff.logdens/skip.configurations as GRID-only controls.
- Original logs show num.threads=1:1 and blas.num.threads=1. An explicit
  blas.num.threads=1 records the intended setting; it is already effective.
