Saved-fit investigation, 2026-10-22

Scope
Read-only reanalysis of pilot and finer-mesh fits for strength_case_0003_Melding,
clustering_case_0003_Melding (delta=2), and strength_case_0003_PSmelding
(gamma=1), plus the saved repeated baseline areal_noise_case_0003_Melding
No fits were replaced, discarded or rerun by this script

Main findings
1. The strongest near-zero variance concentration is the FINER-MESH PSmelding
baseline. Its areal error variance has posterior mean 0.00012745, median
0.00007050, 95% interval [0.00001472,0.00058939], and probability 0.99232 below
0.001. The generating variance is 0.1. The pilot fit has mean 0.018796,
median 0.009479 and probability 0.08978 below 0.001. Both have mode_status=0
and no logged negative Hessian eigenvalues. Therefore those diagnostics alone
do not detect this mesh-sensitive concentration near zero

This does not prove an optimizer failure or an exact boundary solution. The
installed INLA 23.4.24 Gaussian likelihood uses a loggamma prior with parameters
(1,0.00005), as confirmed by inla.models() and the saved likelihood log. Thus
q~Gamma(shape=1,rate=0.00005) and v=1/q~InverseGamma(1,0.00005). The prior's
variance-density mode is 0.000025, median 0.00007213 and P(v<0.001)=0.95123
The finer posterior median is close to that prior median. This resemblance is
consistent with weak information about very small areal noise, but does not
establish prior dominance by itself. No prior was changed or sensitivity fit run
The positive-support continuous prior/posterior assigns no point mass to v=0

2. Delta=2 Melding's finer-mesh mean variance 0.26767 is a different phenomenon
Its areal variance median is 0.11860, 95% interval [0.01422,1.15274], with
P(v<0.001) approximately zero in the saved support, P(v<0.01)=0.00186 and
P(v<0.025)=0.14542. The pilot mean is 0.02715 and median 0.02169. The finer
saved marginal has a broad, irregular high-variance tail on the log-variance
scale, and its inverse-moment mean is dominated by that tail. It is not
concentrating toward zero. The variance-density mode remains about 0.01719,
whereas the peak of the density of log variance corresponds to v about 1.21
These are different quantities because density modes change under nonlinear
transformations. The finer fit reports mode_status=1, one negative Hessian
eigenvalue, an early-stop Hessian message, and a maximum directional SD
correction factor of 29.19. The saved approximation needs numerical review

3. Baseline Melding has a lower areal variance on the finer mesh, but no
comparable concentration below 0.001 in either retained marginal. Pilot mean,
median and 95% interval are 0.03970,0.02591,[0.00944,0.15324]; finer values are
0.02252,0.01763,[0.00760,0.06596]. Both log one negative Hessian eigenvalue
and mode_status=1. Their point-error and nominal spatial-variance posteriors
are well separated from zero. Across all six targets the smallest reported
95% lower endpoint for nominal spatial variance is 0.62468, and for point
error variance 0.04108. Gamma in baseline PSmelding stays close to 1:
pilot mean 0.9412 [0.7147,1.1796], finer mean 0.9216 [0.6949,1.1433]

4. The repeated saved baseline Melding fits demonstrate sensitivity after
mode finding. The strength and areal-noise runs use identical data and seed
Their maximum joint-mode coordinate difference is 0.00002167, log posterior
mode differs by 0.000007765, and maximum saved internal covariance difference
is 0.000002249. Nevertheless areal variance means differ from 0.03970 to
0.01730, the previously reported 0.498 reference posterior SD

Both regularized Hessians have repeated eigenvalues about 8.861, equivalent
to repeated saved covariance eigenvalues about 0.112856. The eigenvectors in
that repeated-eigenvalue subspace rotate substantially (absolute overlaps
0.604 and 0.797), and directional standard-deviation corrections differ:
strength positive correction 3.187 in one direction, versus areal-noise
positive corrections 1.868 and 1.210 in the last two directions. These are
rotated numerical directions, not direct labels for individual parameters
A positive-definite saved covariance matrix is the result after Hessian
correction and does not invalidate the original negative-eigenvalue warning

The strength run's timing-based pred-strategy selection chose plain, whereas
the areal-noise run chose data-rich, despite both having num.threads=1:1
This is another numerical difference; the saved fits alone do not establish
which difference caused the marginal discrepancy

5. Similar saved modes do not prove that either is the true posterior optimum
Several fits contain a retained integration configuration with a larger
original log density than the configuration at the saved mode. The excess is
0.26050 for pilot baseline Melding, 0.40152 for finer baseline Melding,
0.12442 for pilot delta=2 Melding, 0.21691 for finer delta=2 Melding, and
0.60251 for pilot baseline PSmelding. It is zero for finer baseline PSmelding
and the repeated areal-noise Melding baseline. These facts support checking
controlled initializations and the curvature/integration steps together

Interpretation and limits
The saved results distinguish near-zero areal noise in finer PSmelding from
broad, unstable areal-noise tails in Melding. The repeated-baseline comparison
shows that marginal construction can change greatly despite nearly identical
reported modes and corrected covariance matrices. It does not isolate the
causal numerical mechanism or validate an alternative fitted result

Boundary probabilities are calculated from the retained finite-support
log-precision marginal, normalized on a dense grid. Reported zeros/ones refer
to that saved approximation and do not prove exact zero probability outside
its numerical support. Moments/quantiles use the existing validated routine
The plots show density on log10 variance and clearly label this scale; their
peaks are not the variance-density modes. No fresh posterior samples were
needed. No inference about recovery rates is justified from one replicate
