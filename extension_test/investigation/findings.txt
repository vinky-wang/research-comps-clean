EXTENSION NUMERICAL INVESTIGATION
2026-10-22

The investigation found BOTH areal-projection sensitivity and numerical
sensitivity with the data and mesh held fixed. Mesh refinement alone does not
resolve the fitting issues. All original fitted objects and production scripts
were retained. No result was selectively substituted into the simulation study

The existing noise prior also makes very small variances plausible a priori
Consequently, a stable near-zero variance result need not be an optimizer error
These are distinct issues and should not be conflated

1. SAVED PROBLEMATIC FITS

Seven saved fits were inspected: the requested three cases on both meshes,
plus a saved duplicate baseline Melding fit for the repeatability comparison

Baseline PSmelding on the finer mesh is genuinely concentrated near zero in its
saved approximate posterior: areal variance median 0.0000705, 95% interval
[0.0000147, 0.0005894], P(variance < 0.001) = 0.9923
It has mode_status=0 and no logged negative Hessian eigenvalue

The verified Gamma(1,0.00005) precision prior induces an inverse-Gamma variance
prior with median 0.0000721 and P(variance < 0.001) = 0.9512
The posterior resembles that prior scale. This is consistent with weak
information about very small areal variance, but does not prove prior dominance
Neither the prior nor these continuous posteriors places point mass at zero
No prior sensitivity fits or changes were made

The finer delta=2 Melding fit is a different problem: its variance mean 0.2677
comes from a broad, irregular upper tail, with median 0.1186 and 95% interval
[0.0142,1.1527]. It is not near-zero concentration in that saved marginal
Its log records a negative Hessian eigenvalue and an integration-direction SD
correction of 29.19. Its original scalar mean should not be taken at face value
as evidence that the data support much larger noise on a finer mesh

The original repeated Melding baseline modes differ by only 0.0000217 in their
largest internal coordinate, and their log posterior modes differ by 0.0000078
Their covariance matrices are also almost identical, yet areal variance means
differ from 0.03970 to 0.01730. Repaired Hessians have tied eigenvalues; the
corresponding eigenvectors rotate and asymmetric integration corrections change
Timing-based predictor arithmetic also differed: plain versus data-rich
These observations locate substantial sensitivity in curvature/integration as
well as mode finding; they do not identify a single proven causal mechanism

Several saved integration points have higher density than the reported modes
Thus agreement between reported modes is not evidence that either found the
best-supported region. Positive eigenvalues in the saved, repaired covariance
also do not override the negative-eigenvalue messages in the original log

Independent recomputation reproduced all saved noise-variance summaries exactly
The reported discrepancies are not reciprocal-of-mean errors or stale summaries
See posterior/findings.txt, its tables/figures, and methods/methodological_review.txt

2. AREAL PROJECTION

All 25 generating areal means exactly match the mean of their 100 raster cells
A_B row sums are one; vertex counts range from 36-44 on the production mesh to
65-76 on the finer mesh. These actual meshes have no shared-boundary vertices
The requested cases share the same truth, areal data and A_B within resolution

The saved field is known only at 2500 cell centres. Mesh nodal values therefore
require a diagnostic reconstruction. Both nearest-cell and bilinear interpolation
were checked, clamping coordinates to the outermost available centres at edges
Exact FE integration clips mesh triangles to each area and integrates linear
basis functions using fragment areas and centroids. Constant, linear and domain
coverage checks all pass

RMSE against the stored true areal means:
                                      Production mesh    Finer mesh
Equal-vertex A_B, bilinear                    0.04160        0.03247
Exact FE integration, bilinear               0.01674        0.01165
Equal-vertex A_B, nearest cell                0.04378        0.03895
Exact FE integration, nearest cell            0.02992        0.02219

With bilinear nodal reconstruction, the mesh-to-mesh change in the projected
areal averages has RMS 0.04774 for A_B and 0.01012 for exact FE integration
The generating areal-noise SD is sqrt(0.1)=0.3162 for scale comparison

Equal-vertex weighting contributes measurable discretization error. Exact FE
integration improves this diagnostic for both reconstructions. However, it is
exact only for the reconstructed FE surface, not for unobserved continuous truth
These comparisons alone cannot explain or causally attribute the tenfold fitted
variance change. No fitting projector was replaced in this investigation
See projection/findings.txt and projection/projection_area_comparison.csv

3. CONTROLLED OPTIMIZER INVESTIGATION

The plan was recorded before fitting in optimizer_plan.txt
For each of six source fits, three starts used tolerance=0.0001 instead of 0.005:
  - the saved hyperparameter mode
  - that mode with initial areal variance changed to 0.001
  - that mode with initial areal variance changed to 0.1
All use the saved latent-mode starting vector, restart=TRUE and fixed=FALSE
The variance is estimated; those values are starting points only
Two default-call repetitions and one tight saved-start repetition were also run

Data, formula, priors, meshes, A_B, integration strategy, variance correction and
finite-difference step h=0.005 were unchanged. The original INLA 1:1 thread setting
and seed were retained; BLAS/OpenMP thread settings were explicitly set to one
The inner latent-solver tolerance remained unchanged: this is tighter
hyperparameter optimization, not universally higher numerical accuracy

All 21 planned fits completed and were retained. All extracted parameter summaries
passed the implemented finite-value/coherence checks. Nevertheless, one fit has
nonzero mode status and four have logged negative Hessian eigenvalues
Two PSmelding runs evaluated integration points above their reported modes
Those are numerical review flags, even when the returned summaries are finite

Baseline Melding: all three tight starts found closely matching, much higher
log-posterior modes than the originals, by about 3.674 log units on the production
mesh and 3.835 on the finer mesh. Their areal-variance means are about 0.00016-
0.00018, close to zero relative to the generating value 0.1

Delta=2 Melding: initialization still matters on BOTH meshes. The start at 0.001
finds higher-density near-zero solutions; other starts can stay in different
regions. On the production mesh, the log-mode improvements relative to the
original are approximately 0.00016,3.874 and 1.013 for saved, 0.001 and 0.1 starts
On the finer mesh they are 4.020,4.020 and0.543. Even mode_status=0 and an absence
of negative-eigenvalue warnings can accompany a lower-density local result

Baseline PSmelding: production-mesh starts remain sensitive. The 0.001 start
finds a mode 2.911 log units above the original, with area-variance mean 0.000156
The other two starts remain near the original lower-density region with means
about 0.020. The saved-start run has mode_status=1; the 0.1-start run logs a
negative Hessian eigenvalue. Both evaluate an integration point above their
reported modes. On the finer mesh all three starts remain near zero, with
means 0.000122-0.000140 and nearly identical log-mode values

REPEATABILITY
The two new default-call repetitions are identical in modes, reported parameters
and prediction means. They reproduce the earlier areal-noise baseline result,
not the original strength-run marginal. This does not erase the original
repeatability discrepancy

The tighter saved-start baseline Melding repetition is close but not bitwise
identical: prediction-mean RMS difference 5.84e-5, maximum parameter-mean change
0.00368 original posterior SD, and log-mode difference 0.000117
Areal-variance means are 0.0001773 and0.0001725. Both have mode_status=0 and no
negative-eigenvalue log message. Runtime-selected arithmetic remains a possible
source of small residual differences even with fixed R seeds and one thread

These findings do not prove global optimality or determine posterior mass in
competing regions. A higher mode is useful evidence of an inadequate previous
optimization, but selecting the highest mode alone does not validate the full
posterior marginal or integrate possible alternative modes
No result was chosen to replace an original or used to revise simulation scores
See reports/optimizer_runs.csv, optimizer_pairwise.csv,
optimizer_direction_diagnostics.csv and optimizer_areal_variance.png

4. IMPLICATIONS BEFORE THE FULL STUDY

A. Do not rely on mode_status alone, and do not regard a single saved-mode
restart or smaller tolerance as a complete fix. Use a common, documented
initialization and validation protocol across models and scenarios; inspect
higher-density regions, integration diagnostics and marginal stability together
The competing regions for delta=2 Melding and production-mesh PSmelding merit
further hyperparameter profiling/integration checks before production

B. Treat exact FE areal integration as a candidate diagnostic or documented
methodological change, not a silent replacement for Zhong's equal-vertex rule
This investigation demonstrates its improved projection of reconstructed truth;
it did not refit models with that operator or establish that doing so resolves
the numerical issues

C. Keep the current priors if required for reproduction, but explicitly report
near-zero areal-variance behavior and its relationship to the prior scale
Changing priors would change the fitting specification and has not been done
Near-zero concentration cannot simply be discarded as a failed fit

D. Recheck a full-resolution pilot using any finalized common fitting protocol
before the 100-replicate study. This one replicate cannot establish recovery,
coverage or model superiority, and the current investigation does not certify
production readiness
